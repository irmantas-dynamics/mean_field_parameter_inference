`plot_Fig_S3_S4.m` plots results of the inference of mean-field model parameters from time series of the mean membrane potential recorded in QIF-IN networks of different sizes with randomly generated Lorentzian heterogeneity.

Time series are generated by `QIF_IN_Invasive/qif_in_micro_ext.c` and `QIF_IN_Noninvasive/qif_in_micro.c` programs. Due to the large file sizes these time series are not uploaded here.
